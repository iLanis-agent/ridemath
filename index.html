The sticker audit

Every pound over 170 costs a little range, rolling roads take 15 percent, and real hills take a third. The sticker rider never met your commute.

The watt-hour truth

Eco sips 8 watt-hours a mile; turbo pours 25. The same 500 Wh battery is a 62-mile battery or a 20-mile battery depending on one thumb.

The charge clock

Nameplate watts do not finish the job - the last 15 percent crawls. An empty 500 Wh pack on a 100 W brick is an overnight, not a lunch break.

What it never does

It never quotes the marketing range, never sells you a bigger battery, and never pretends February is July. The derates are labeled norms; your odometer keeps the honest ledger.

Ride Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
